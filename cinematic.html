LAIKA

Animated Short Film · Sci-Fi / Psychological Drama

Isolation · Human-AI dynamics · Purpose

A woman alone on an alien planet, her only company a vocal AI she never asked for. A story about solitude, purpose, and what it means to share a fate. Loosely inspired by Laika, the dog aboard Sputnik 2.

A boy, a camera with two films left, and a dog he never got to say goodbye to. A dream logic short about grief, memory, and the small thoughts that fill a whole life.
